# WonderNet — Vision

WonderNet is a local-first, open-source educational and entertainment environment designed for small, affordable computers—starting with the Raspberry Pi 4B—and phones or tablets already in people's homes.

## Who is it for?

- **Early learners:** phonemic awareness, systematic phonics, decoding, counting and reading aloud.
- **Elementary learners:** literacy, mathematics, science, digital safety and creativity.
- **Middle-school learners:** programming, networking, problem solving and practical skill exploration.
- **High-school learners:** hands-on trades, technical topics, career exploration and simulated cyber investigations.
- **Adults and lifelong learners:** refresher education, career development, practical skills, reading and creative hobbies.
- **Families, educators and community groups:** eventually, curated offline libraries, classroom assignments and private games.

Difficulty level and visual treatment are **separate**. Older users should never be forced into an infantilizing interface to review foundational skills.

## Experience principles

1. **Direct navigation.** A conventional homepage, universal search, bookmarks and continue-where-you-left-off—not a character-controlled hub.
2. **Offline capabilities first.** Once installed, core experiences operate without external services or telemetry.
3. **Entertainment is its own destination.** Games need not teach something and are never locked behind homework.
4. **Meaningful accessibility.** Keyboard and touch, captions, reduced motion, reading support, high contrast, alternative play mechanics.
5. **A safe place to experiment.** Especially for future student programming and cyber labs, learner code must remain isolated from the platform and other users.
6. **Modular, but reviewed.** Curriculum, media and games can grow through approved packages without compromising the core.
7. **Privacy for children and families.** Minimize data, avoid public matchmaking, do not use targeted advertising.

## Illustrative experiences (not yet built)

- Phonics: listen to /c/ /a/ /t/, blend sounds, then read a decodable sentence.
- Cybersecurity: investigate a fictional login alert with synthetic logs.
- Trades: read a birdhouse blueprint and measure virtual material.
- Library: import a properly licensed book and assign it to a reading list.
- Arcade: explore an original farming sim or solve a cooperative mystery.
- WonderParty: join a private drawing/trivia room on a local phone.

## What WonderNet is not

It is **not** a safe child-facing product yet; it is **not** an internet proxy, an unsupervised real-system hacking lab, or a collection of third-party copyrighted games. This repo documents a long-term vision alongside a very early technical prototype.

The roadmap separates current work from ideas. Propose refinements via GitHub issues.
