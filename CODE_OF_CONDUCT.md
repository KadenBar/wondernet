# Community Code of Conduct

WonderNet is being built so people of different ages, abilities and backgrounds can learn, play and contribute.

## Our expectations

- Be kind, constructive and patient with learners and first-time contributors.
- Critique ideas and code, not people's identities, disabilities or experience.
- Make feedback actionable. Respect different learning and communication needs.
- Protect privacy: do not post personally identifying information about children or other contributors.
- Ask consent before publishing another person's creative work or correspondence.
- Respect contributors' time; an idea being suggested does not make it a promised feature.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexually inappropriate comments directed at others, intimidation, doxxing, malicious disruption, and sharing exploit details that jeopardize learners.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, and report serious abuse to the appropriate service. Before a larger community launch, we will publish a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting contact.

For **security vulnerabilities**, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public community reports.
