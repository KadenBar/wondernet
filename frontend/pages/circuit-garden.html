YOUR LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

How to grow a circuit

⚡
Start with the battery

It sends power into any wire facing the same way.

↻
Rotate the pieces

Tap or press Enter or Space. Wires must connect end to end.

✿
Make the flowers bloom

Power every flower to finish the garden. The green glow shows what's connected.

Keyboard tip: Use arrow keys to move between pieces. No time limit and no penalties for trying.
