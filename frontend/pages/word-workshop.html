THE WORKSHOP RULES

How to play

1

Look at the picture and read the clue.

2

Put letter tiles into the spaces in the right order.

3

Press Build this word! and collect your star stamp.

On phones, touch a letter and slide your finger to a word space. If dragging is difficult, select Choose a specific space, tap a letter, then tap the destination. Ordinary taps and keyboard buttons still work. Progress is in memory only.
