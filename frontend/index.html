HOW WE BUILD

Useful first. Delightful always.

WonderNet should feel like a website you can simply use. No virtual town to cross, no homework required before play, and no barriers between someone and the thing they came to do.

01

Fast to navigate

Clear menus, quick access to favorites and, eventually, one-click return to your last activity.

02

Safe to experiment

Curious coders get intentionally isolated labs, not access to other people's information.

03

Made for different minds

Keyboard access, adjustable experiences, reading support and accessible games are design requirements.
